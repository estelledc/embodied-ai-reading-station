# Embodied AI: Zero to One — rights notice

This notice defines `EAI-LICENSE-MAP-1.0.0` for project code, project editorial material, and paper-related assets. It separates that governed scope into four classes; dependencies, fonts, and vendored libraries remain outside this mapping, and a license declared for one class does not relicense another class.

| Stable asset class | Declared expression | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `project-code` | [`MIT`](https://opensource.org/license/mit) | Project-authored site code, build scripts, tests, and configuration. See [LICENSE](LICENSE). |
| `project-notes` | [`CC-BY-4.0`](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | Project-authored explanatory notes and editorial text. Attribution and change notices are required by that license. |
| `project-generated-images` | [`CC-BY-4.0`](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | Policy class only for images separately proven to be project-generated and licensable. The current provenance v2 fields cannot establish that legal fact, so no asset inherits this declaration automatically. |
| `third-party-paper-materials` | [`NOASSERTION`](https://spdx.github.io/spdx-spec/v2.3/package-information/) | Papers, parsed paper material, extracted figures, thumbnails, and every generated-asset record without separate licensable-rights evidence remain subject to their respective rights holders and source-specific terms. |

`NOASSERTION` is a rights-status marker, not a license grant. Neither the root MIT license nor the project CC declaration covers third-party paper material.

Dependencies, fonts, and vendored libraries retain their own upstream licenses. Package metadata and upstream distributions remain the authoritative source for those terms.

The machine-readable mapping is published by the data manifests. Its provenance bindings and fail-closed update process are defined by `EAI-PROVENANCE-2.0.0` in [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md).

The copyright-holder identity, the project's ability to grant every declared right, and the legal completeness of this notice have not received independent legal review; their evidence status is `UNVERIFIED_OWNER_OR_LEGAL`.
